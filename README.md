# Adam Friedl

Two careers doing the same thing: building systems that help people get to better outcomes. The first was in law and access-to-justice work — legal aid, community court operations, technology for self-represented litigants. ## What I care about

I'm drawn to work where craft meets human stakes, where how well a system is built decides whether someone gets what they need. I use AI tooling daily, and I care about how it's changing the work and the people who do it, for better and for worse.

Day to day, that means platform engineering: GCP, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD, and observability.
